## FlyWire FAFB v783 data

The whole-connectome package used by the boss is derived from FlyWire FAFB
v783 connectivity and annotations. The upstream package identifies the
derived connectome data as CC BY 4.0 and requires attribution.

Primary scientific references:

- Dorkenwald S. et al. Neuronal wiring diagram of an adult brain. Nature 634,
  124-138 (2024). DOI: 10.1038/s41586-024-07558-y
- Schlegel P. et al. Whole-brain annotation and multi-connectome cell typing
  of Drosophila. Nature 634, 139-152 (2024).
- Shiu P.K. et al. A Drosophila computational brain model reveals
  sensorimotor processing. Nature (2024).

FlySwarm does not claim that the wiring diagram alone constitutes a complete
biological or cognitive simulation of a fruit fly.
